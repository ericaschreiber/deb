About Deb

"The deepest transformation begins when we stop protecting ourselves and remember who we are." — Deb

For more than twenty years, Deb Katz has helped leaders see and face their default patterns, then consciously choose new ways of being and doing. Leaders rely on Deb to deliver challenging, actionable feedback with care.

Deb coaches, facilitates teams, leads Signal Forums and Intensives, trains and supervises coaches, and offers keynotes. She is a founding member of The Signal Institute and former founding member of The Conscious Leadership Group.

In her political activist days, Deb mobilized tens of thousands of Californians to reverse draconian higher education budget cuts. What she learned shapes every client engagement: Transformation doesn't come from a reactive state. Nothing actually changes until leaders stop blaming and start examining their own part in creating what they're complaining about.

During a decade as a cardiac and hospice nurse, Deb mastered the distinction between urgency and emergency, while leading people through the most significant life thresholds. She brings that same steadiness to the threshold every leader eventually faces: the one between who they've been and who they can become.

Years of training with RainbowHawk and WindEagle of the Ehama Institute taught her that beneath the noise of an identity trying to secure itself through accomplishments is a truer, wilder nature longing to be remembered. Earth-based wisdom returns us to something feral and essential: an internal, utterly trustworthy compass that never stops pointing true.

Deb's expertise is in facilitating sustainable team and culture transformation. She supports leaders to see their part in creating the dynamics they're complaining about, then guides them to the brave place most teams never reach: saying the hard things to one another with care and without armor.

Deb is based in Portland, Oregon, where she might be on the river, in the garden, or getting into shenanigans with her pup Mufasa.
